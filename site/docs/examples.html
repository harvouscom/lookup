Three made-up apps built with Lookup, each opening full screen. A church management app, a nonprofit sign-up and a church website builder.

Each example is a working app using the real widget and live data, in demo mode so nothing you share is sent. They open full screen on the homepage.

Kinship, a church management appReact with controlled inputs. Add a partner church and watch React's state fill in.Harvest Table, a food pantry networkA plain HTML sign-up that registers churches as distribution sites, posting church_id with the form.Steeple, a church website builderPick your church and a live website preview builds itself from its details.

#What each one shows

ExampleShows
KinshipReact controlled inputs, church-select and church-clear events, the share panel
Harvest Tablename and required in a plain form, service times feeding distribution hours
SteepleFilling a live preview, <church-service-times>, theming to a product's own look

Every example uses client="lookup-demo", so corrections are checked but never sent to Here's My Church.
